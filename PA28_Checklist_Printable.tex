\subsection*{External Checks}
\begin{checklist}
\item Check documents are current and correct.
\item Visually check the aircraft’s general condition.
\item Remove tie-down ropes, pitot head cover, chocks and any external control locks.
\item In cold weather, remove even small accumulations of frost, ice or snow from the aircraft.
\item Check that the aircraft is parked in a free area, so that it may easily move onto the taxyway.
\item Check that a fire extinguisher is available.
\end{checklist}

\section*{Emergencies}

\subsection*{Engine Failure After Takeoff}
\textit{If the engine fails after take-off you should (as soon as you notice the loss of power):}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Lower the Nose and Maintain the Aircraft’s Gliding Speed
\item Close the Throttle
\item Select the Most Suitable Landing Area, Compatible With Obstructions and Wind
  \begin{checklist}
  \item Direction; Preferably Straight Ahead or Within 30\textdegree{} Either Side
  \end{checklist}
\item Maintain Speed and Use Flap as Necessary
\item If Time Permits: - Mixture to I.C.O., Fuel Off, Fuel Pump Off, Magnetos Off, Door
  \begin{checklist}
  \item Unlocked and Harnesses Tight, Mayday Call, Master Switch Off
  \end{checklist}
\item After Touchdown Leave the Aircraft as Quickly as Possible
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Crash Drill}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Mixture I.C.O, Fuel Pump Off
\item Fuel Selector Off
\item Magnetos Off
\item Brakes Off
\item Harnesses Very Tight
\item Hatches Unlatched
\item Master Switch Off (After Electrical Services are No Longer Required)
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Engine Fire During Start}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Starter - Crank Engine
\item Mixture - Idle Cut Off
\item Throttle - Open
\item Electric Fuel Pump - Off
\item Fuel Selector - Off
\item Abandon Aircraft if Fire Continues
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Engine Fire In The Air}

\item Fuel Selector - Off
\item Throttle - Closed
\item Mixture - Idle Cut-Off
\item Electric Fuel Pump - Check Off
\item Heater and Defroster - Off
\item Proceed with Power Off Landing Procedure
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Electrical Fire In The Air}

\item Vents - Open
\item Cabin Heat - Off
\item Land as soon as Practicable.
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Cabin Fire In The Air}

\end{numberedchecklist}

\subsection*{Forced Landing Without Power}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Adopt Glide and Trim for Correct Gliding Speed
\item Check Wind Direction
\item Select Landing Area and 1000' Area
\item Plan Circuit
\item Complete Failure Checks:
  \begin{checklist}
  \item Switch Tanks
  \item Electric Fuel Pump On
  \item Mixture Rich
  \item Carb. Heat Change Position
  \item Magnetos on Both
  \item Primer Locked
  \end{checklist}
\item Mayday Call on Radio
\item Complete Crash Drill
\item Use Full Flap for the Touchdown if Possible
\item Apply Heavy Braking
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Forced Landing With Power}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Assess the Time Available and Check Fuel State, Hours of Daylight and Rate of
  \begin{checklist}
  \item Weather Deterioration
  \end{checklist}
\item Check Wind Direction
\item Select Suitable Landing Area
\item Carry Out an Inspection Run at Slow Safe Cruising Speed; Initially at 500' to Check
  \begin{checklist}
  \item for Obstructions and Secondly at 100' Over the Landing Area to Reassess the
  \item Approach Path, Landing Surface and Overshoot Area (if time permits)
  \end{checklist}
\item Set up For a Short or Soft Field Landing (as appropriate)
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Ditching}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Set up Minimum Rate of Descent (75 Kts)
\item Mayday Call
\item Secure or Jettison Heavy Objects
\item With High Winds and Heavy Seas: Approach Into Wind.
  \begin{checklist}
  \item With Light Winds and Heavy Swells: Approach Parallel to Swells.
  \end{checklist}
\item Unlatch the Door
\item Touchdown in a Level Attitude
\item Evacuate Through the Cabin Door; if Necessary Open Window to Equalise Pressure
\end{numberedchecklist}

\textbf{Notes:}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Ideally, land ahead and either side of a small ship.
\item Do not inflate life jackets in the cockpit.
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Low-Voltage/Electrical Failure}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Check Ammeter, if Reading Zero, Alternator Off
\item Reset Circuit Breaker
\item Alternator On
\item Reduce Electrical Load
\end{numberedchecklist}

\textbf{Notes:}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item If total electrical failure occurs, keep an extra good lookout. Land when safe to do so,
  \begin{checklist}
  \item clear the runway as quickly as possible and phone ATC at once.
  \end{checklist}
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Radio Failure}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item Check Correct Frequency
\item Check Squelch Setting (if applicable)
\item Check Volume
\item Check Microphone Plug Secure
\item Check Circuit Breaker
\item Try to Communicate Using the Other PTT Switch
\end{numberedchecklist}

\textbf{Notes:}
\begin{numberedchecklist}
\item If the radio is unserviceable: flash the landing light on approach and land on visual
  \begin{checklist}
  \item signals.
  \end{checklist}
\item If flying outside of the circuit, follow lost comm procedure from AIP.
\end{numberedchecklist}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{Uncertain of Position / Lost Procedure}

\subsubsection*{Uncertain of Position}
Inform ATC immediately on the frequency you are in communication with.
Check flight plan, log, compass and heading for gross errors; calculate DR position; from
flight time decide radius of circle of uncertainty; mark on map using DR position as Centre;
estimate new heading from DR position to regain destination or track. Utilise Nav aids
including moving map if available.

\subsubsection*{Lost Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item Radio Available
  \begin{checklist}
  \item Check frequency selection; declare that you are lost; make maximum use of aids
  \item available; consider increasing height to improve range (taking account of controlled
  \item airspace). R/T procedure: urgency or distress call to obtain fix. You are no longer lost.
  \item Follow the ATC instructions in full.
  \end{checklist}
\item Radio Not Available
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\roman*.]
  \item Check remaining fuel and calculate endurance; consider daylight remaining, weather
    \begin{checklist}
    \item tendency, nature of terrain in relation to probable position, minimum safe altitude; recheck flight log for errors; switch transponder, if available, to emergency code.
    \end{checklist}
  \item Map reading from ground to map and try to identify the likely area from the nature of
    \begin{checklist}
    \item the terrain before checking other features. Look for prominent features in the distance
    \item as well as nearby; adhere to a calculated heading and keep map properly orientated.
    \end{checklist}
  \item Decide on a positive course of action; relative merits of square search, flight towards
    \begin{checklist}
    \item prominent or line feature outside of circle of uncertainty; consider precautionary
    \item landing (see Ex 17) if you are short of fuel or are unable to continue the flight.
    \end{checklist}
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\textit{End of Emergency Checklist}
