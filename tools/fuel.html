Part-NCO minimum final reserve (aeroplanes, per CAA SS05): day remaining in sight of and returning to the aerodrome 10 min; day 30 min; night 45 min, at normal cruising altitude — regard these as minimums. Defaults are the most conservative (best power, 75%); reserve and contingency are pilot/operator policy. Reserve, contingency, mixture and power are edited above, per flight. Reserve and alternate fuel are computed at the cruise fuel flow.
