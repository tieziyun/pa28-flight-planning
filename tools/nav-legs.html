How it works

Show the maths

A = wind direction − track. WCA = arcsin[(wind ÷ TAS) × sin A]. True heading = track + WCA. Groundspeed = TAS × cos WCA − wind × cos A. Time = distance ÷ groundspeed × 60.

Check: wind from the right of track turns the nose right (heading goes up); wind from the left turns it left. A straight headwind gives zero correction and GS = TAS − wind.

PLOG contents per CAA Safety Sense 05: timings and headings for each leg, radio frequencies, planned altitudes, safety altitudes, fuel plan. MEF on VFR charts is the highest terrain/obstruction in the area with no margin — it is not a minimum safe altitude. Sense-check headings (e.g. 030° vs 300°).

Training aid only. Cross-check with your CRP-5 and PLOG, and use the forecast wind for your planned altitude.
